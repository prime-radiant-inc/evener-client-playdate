# Onboarding narration

All spoken narration uses the local macOS Samantha synthesized voice at 165 words per minute. The dictated demonstration phrase also used synthesized speech. The setup sound is an actual same-run room recording; narration stops before both chirp blocks.

## 00:00:00,000 — A recorded walkthrough

This walkthrough uses real Playdate and Hub captures. Narration and the demonstration speech are synthesized. Waiting periods are edited.

## 00:00:10,000 — Before you start

Build Evener in setup-only mode with your own OpenAI key. Keep that bundle private. Use USB Data Disk mode to copy Evener dot P D X into Games, then eject and open it.

## 00:00:24,000 — Listen

Open Receive setup. On an existing installation, find it under Actions in the system menu.

## 00:00:30,000 — Create an invitation

In the Hub, open Settings, Mobile app, then Pair using sound. Check the Hub address, choose Tailscale, and create an invitation.

## 00:00:44,000 — Setup sound / production take

Play the setup sound beside your Playdate.

## 00:00:55,300 — Join the Hub’s tailnet

If enrollment is needed, scan the Tailscale code on the Playdate. On your phone, sign in and authorize the device on the Hub’s tailnet. Phone approval was not recorded.

## 00:01:09,300 — Approval / earlier recorded take

This approval example is from an earlier take. Compare all four lines on your own screens. When every group matches, approve in the Hub.

## 00:01:25,300 — Approval confirmed

The Hub confirms approval. Keep the Playdate connected.

## 00:01:31,300 — Save

Review the destination on the Playdate, then press A to save it. The client connects and opens your sessions.

## 00:01:41,300 — Find a conversation

Select a session with the crank or D-pad, then press A to open it.

## 00:01:47,300 — Dictate

Hold A and start speaking immediately. The microphone captures your words.

## 00:01:53,300 — Review

Release A to finish recording. Wait for the final text to be saved, then read it. The message has not been sent yet.

## 00:02:08,300 — Send

Press A separately to send the reviewed message. An active session receives it in its queue.

## 00:02:16,300 — Read

The reply appears in the conversation. Use the crank to read through it. This is the recorded test exchange.
